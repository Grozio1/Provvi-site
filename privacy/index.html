Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wortman IP Holdings LLC, doing business as Provvi, handles information when You use the Provvi application.

Summary

Provvi has no user accounts and no login. We do not ask for Your name, email address, or phone number, and we have no way to contact You.

Your grocery lists, shopping history, budget, and preferences are stored on Your device, not on our servers.

To estimate prices, we send item names, Your ZIP code or approximate location, and Your selected stores to an artificial intelligence service. We do not send anything that identifies You, because we do not have anything that identifies You.

We do not sell Your information. We do not share it with advertisers, retailers, brands, or data brokers. We have no advertising, no affiliate relationships, and no retailer partnerships.

The rest of this document explains all of that in detail.

Information We Collect

Information stored only on Your Device

The following is created by You and stored locally on Your Device. It is not transmitted to Us and We cannot access it:

  • Your grocery lists and their contents
  • Your shopping trip history, including items, quantities, prices, stores, and dates
  • Your budget amount and spending totals
  • Your dietary restrictions and preferences
  • Your generated meal plans
  • Your ZIP code and selected store preferences
  • Any price corrections You record
  • Cached price estimates and application settings

If You delete the Application, this information is deleted with it.

Information sent to Our servers

When You use a feature that requires a price estimate, a meal plan, or an insight, the Application sends the following to Our server, which forwards the relevant portions to an artificial intelligence Service Provider:

  • The item names on Your list
  • Your ZIP code, or Your approximate location if You have granted location permission
  • The names of the stores You have selected
  • Your dietary restrictions and preferences, where relevant to the request
  • Any question You type into the "Ask" feature
  • Your Device Identifier
  • Your IP address, which is inherent to any internet request

Your Device Identifier and IP address are used solely to apply usage limits and to prevent abuse of the Service. They are held only for the duration of a rolling limit window and are not used to build a profile of You.

Information We do not collect

We want to be specific about this, because generic privacy policies often claim otherwise:

  • We do not collect Your name.
  • We do not collect Your email address or phone number.
  • We do not collect Your street address.
  • We do not have user accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • We do not collect payment or card details. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google and We never see Your payment information.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or tracking technologies.
  • We do not track You across other apps or websites.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Generate price estimates, brand suggestions, meal plans, and spending insights that You have requested
  • Identify grocery stores near You, if You have granted location permission
  • Apply usage limits fairly and protect the Service from abuse
  • Diagnose crashes and technical errors so We can fix them

We do not use Your information for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and We have no ability to contact You.

Use of Artificial Intelligence

Provvi uses artificial intelligence to generate price estimates, product and brand suggestions, meal plans, and spending insights.

When You use these features, the information listed under "Information sent to Our servers" is transmitted to Anthropic, PBC, the provider of the Claude AI service, for processing. Anthropic's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy, which We encourage You to review at anthropic.com/privacy.

No information that identifies You is included in these requests, because We do not collect any.

AI-generated price estimates are approximations based on general market data. They may not reflect actual current prices at any specific store, and they are provided for planning and comparison purposes only. You should verify prices at the store before making purchasing decisions. Please see Our Terms and Conditions for the full description of the limits of this information.

Location Information

If You grant permission, the Application uses Your Device's approximate location to find grocery stores near You and to estimate distance. We request approximate rather than precise location, because approximate location is sufficient for this purpose.

Location is used at the time of Your request and is not stored on Our servers or used to track Your movements.

You may decline location permission, or withdraw it at any time in Your Device settings. If You do, the Application uses a ZIP code that You enter instead, and all other features continue to work normally.

Service Providers

We use a small number of third parties to operate the Service. Each receives only what it needs to perform its function:

  • Anthropic, PBC — AI price estimates, meal plans, and insights. Receives item names, ZIP code or approximate location, store names, dietary preferences, and questions You type.

  • Google (Places) — finding grocery stores near You. Receives Your ZIP code or approximate location.

  • Railway — hosting Our server. Receives requests routed through Our server, including IP address.

  • Sentry — crash and error reporting. Receives device model, operating system version, application version, technical error details, and Device Identifier.

  • Apple and Google — processing purchases. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred, not Your payment details.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell Your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under applicable United States state privacy laws.

We do not share Your information with retailers, grocery chains, food brands, advertisers, or data brokers. Provvi has no retailer partnerships, no affiliate arrangements, no referral fees, and no advertising. This is a deliberate structural choice, and it is described in Our Terms and Conditions.

We may disclose information only where We are legally required to do so, such as in response to a valid legal request from a public authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Company, Our users, or the public.

If the Company is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, any information We hold may be transferred as part of that transaction. We would provide notice before Your information became subject to a different privacy policy.

Deleting Your Data

Because Your information is stored on Your Device, You control it directly.

From within the Application: tap the gear icon, then select "Delete Account & Data". This permanently deletes all Your lists, trip history, budget, preferences, meal plans, and cached data from Your Device. The action is immediate and cannot be undone.

By deleting the Application: removing Provvi from Your Device removes the data stored with it.

We do not maintain an account or profile associated with You, so there is no server-side record for Us to delete on request. The only information that reaches Our servers is transient: price cache entries that expire automatically, usage-limit counters that reset hourly, and crash reports. None of these are linked to Your identity.

If You have questions about deletion, You may contact Us at the address below.

Data Retention

  • On Your Device: retained until You delete it, or delete the Application.
  • Price cache on Our server: automatically expires within 24 hours. Not linked to You.
  • Usage-limit counters: held for the duration of a rolling one-hour window, then discarded.
  • Server logs: retained for a short period for security and troubleshooting, then discarded.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): retained in accordance with Sentry's standard retention period, typically 90 days.

Security

We use HTTPS for all communication between the Application and Our servers, and We store Our credentials securely.

Please note that data stored locally on an Android Device is not encrypted at rest by default at the operating-system level. We recommend You use Your Device's own screen lock and encryption features.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. While We use commercially reasonable means to protect information, We cannot guarantee absolute security.
